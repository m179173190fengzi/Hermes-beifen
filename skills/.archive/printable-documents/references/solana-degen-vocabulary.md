# Solana Degen / Crypto Narrative English

Core vocabulary for understanding English-language crypto Twitter meme coin narratives.
Completely separate domain from general internet memes.

## Real Narrative Examples (with translation)

### Alpha call (gem call)
> "GM frens. Just found a gem on Solana. CA: xyz... MC only 50K, LP locked, dev only 2%. This could be the next blueberry in the AI meta. Did my DD and aped a bag. NFA DYOR."

翻译：早上好朋友们，刚找到一个Solana宝石。合约地址xxx，市值才5万，流动性锁了，项目方才2%。这可能是AI叙事下下一个旗舰币。我做了研究已经冲了。不是投资建议，自己做研究。

### Rug warning
> "Stay away from this one. Dev wallet has 15%, top 10 hold 60%. Liquidity not locked. This is a honeypot, you can buy but can't sell. Let it die."

翻译：远离这个。项目方持15%，前10大占60%。流动性未锁。这是貔貅盘，你能买但卖不了。让它自己死吧。

### Narrative hype
> "Bro this is NOT just another dog coin. The ticker is $TRUMP but it's about financial sovereignty. The community is insane. 1000 holders in 10 minutes. Dev renounced. If you can't see the vision you're ngmi."

翻译：兄弟这不是又一个狗币。代码是$TRUMP但叙事是金融自主。社区爆发，10分钟1000个持有人。项目方已放弃管理权。你看不懂就不配赚钱。
