## Common Meme Sentence Templates

- "Nobody: / Absolutely nobody: \n  Me: ..." — 没人说话时铺垫
- "I'm in this photo and I don't like it" — 被说中了
- "This is fine" — 表面没事实际已崩
- "Hol up / Say sike right now" — 等一下？
- "Ratio + L + you fell off" — 网络掐架三连
- "Am I the only one who..." — 只有我觉得？
- "It's free real estate" — 不要白不要
- "We have X at home" — 平替/盗版

## Meme Grammar Rules

1. Omit articles: "Man goes to store" not "A man goes to the store"
2. Present tense narration: "Guy walks into bar, says..."
3. Deliberate bad grammar: "Me when I..." not "I when I..."
4. Incomplete sentences: "When you realize..." unsaid conclusion
5. ALL CAPS emphasis: "THIS IS FINE"
6. Repetition emphasis: "Why Why Why"
7. Sentence-final "tho": "It's good tho" (softening)
